WHO WE ARE

About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organisation founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla.

Based in Chhattisgarh and working across India, the foundation focuses on creating lasting social impact through the collective efforts of professionals, volunteers and partners.

Its initiatives address important areas including food support, education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and skill development.

🌱

Our Vision

To contribute towards a more inclusive, compassionate and empowered society through collective action and meaningful initiatives.
